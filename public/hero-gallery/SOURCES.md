# Hero Gallery Sources

Images are downloaded from The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access collection.

- `manet-monet-family-garden.webp`: Edouard Manet, "The Monet Family in Their Garden at Argenteuil", object 436965, public domain.
- `vangogh-flowering-orchard.webp`: Vincent van Gogh, "The Flowering Orchard", object 436527, public domain.
- `pissarro-tuileries-spring.webp`: Camille Pissarro, "The Garden of the Tuileries on a Spring Morning", object 437313, public domain.

Collection pages use this format:
`https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/{object_id}`
